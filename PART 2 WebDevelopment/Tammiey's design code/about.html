About Tammie's Design

Tammie's Design is a bespoke tailoring business based in Potchefstroom, South Africa. We specialise in creating custom-made garments that celebrate South African cultural textile traditions while delivering contemporary, beautifully crafted pieces.

Our Story

With a passion for traditional craftsmanship and an appreciation for South African cultural heritage, Tammie's Design was founded to provide personalised tailoring services that honour cultural authenticity while meeting modern design expectations.

Each garment is created with meticulous attention to detail, combining traditional techniques with contemporary aesthetics to produce unique, individually tailored pieces that reflect each client's personality and style.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide exceptional bespoke tailoring services that honour South African cultural heritage while delivering beautifully crafted, individually tailored garments that reflect each client's unique style and personality.

Our Vision

To be a leading bespoke tailoring service that celebrates South African cultural traditions and empowers individuals to express their identity through custom-designed, premium-quality garments.
